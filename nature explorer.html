Forest Adventure

The forest as a character: It is mysterious, beautiful, and sometimes menacing, with hidden paths and places where it is easy to get lost. A journey or quest: The protagonist travels through the woods toward a goal, such as finding something, escaping, or reaching a destination. Encounters: Animals, hidden creatures, magical beings, or strangers appear along the way and either help or threaten the hero. Obstacles and survival: Rivers, storms, darkness, and scarce food or shelter force problem-solving.

River Journey

Mountain View

Mountains are tall landforms that give us amazing views and are home to many unique plants and animals.

Wildlife Zone

1.Natural Vegetation Cover It contains natural, often undisturbed vegetation (grassland, savanna, or forest depending on location) which serves as habitat and food source for the animals within it. 2. Diverse Animal Species It houses a variety of wild animals — both herbivores (e.g., elephants, antelopes, zebras) and carnivores (e.g., lions, leopards) — living in conditions close to their natural habitat. 3. Water Sources Wildlife zones usually have rivers, streams, lakes, or artificial waterholes to provide drinking water for the animals, especially important during dry seasons.

Nature Explorer Checklist

  • Observe plants carefully
  • Do not disturb animals
  • Keep the environment clean
  • Carry a water bottle
